Packing List

Nineteen days across four climates, packed into four bags that keep changing jobs: the Away gets checked and mostly sits in hotel rooms, the Talon flies as your carry-on and then becomes your base camp bag, an Osprey daypack works the cities and the park, and a sling holds the things that must never leave you. Climate figures are October norms: Xi'an 50–68 °F (10–20 °C) with rain; Lhasa 63–73 °F (17–23 °C) day, 41–55 °F (5–13 °C) night; Everest Base Camp up to 50 °F (10 °C) by day falling to about 21 °F (−6 °C) at night, with strong wind and severe UV. The EBC tent camp is dismantled at the end of October — your 7 October night is inside the window.

Bag specs from Away and Osprey. Allowances: Korean Air economy US–Asia, 2 × 23 kg checked at 158 cm linear, cabin 10 kg across both pieces; China Eastern domestic economy, 20 kg checked at 100 × 60 × 40 cm; China Railway, 20 kg and 160 cm linear per adult. Confirm the China Eastern sector against your actual ticket — a through-fare may carry the international allowance instead.
