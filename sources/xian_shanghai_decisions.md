## Disneyland verdict
**Dated tickets, not an annual pass.** Live prices: Pearl ¥1,599 / Jade ¥2,849 / Diamond ¥4,699.
- Pearl is **weekdays only** — weekends and public holidays blocked — so Sunday 11 Oct is out.
- 3 park days: tickets ~¥1,300 vs Pearl ¥1,599 -> tickets win by ¥299.
- 4 days including Sun 11: Pearl can't cover the Sunday -> tickets win by ~¥348.
- 4 all-weekday days (Mon 12 – Thu 15): pass wins by ~¥125, the only shape where it does.
- Early Park Entry is **Diamond-only**; no pass tier discounts Premier Access, hotel rooms or tickets, and pass holders can't buy the ticket bundles.
- Merchandise coupons (2 x ~20% off per week) are the only lever that could flip it — ~¥1,500 of spend would be needed to overcome the 3-day gap.
- Foreign passport holders must **activate on-site** at Main Entrance Guest Services.
- Buy official first; Klook/Trip.com only as a card/SMS fallback, and Premier Access official-only.

Preferred shape: city day Sun 11 Oct, park Mon 12 – Wed 14, optional half-day Thu 15 before the 18:25 PVG departure. Note the Urban Planning Exhibition Center closes **Wednesdays** and its booking for Sun 11 Oct opens 4 Oct, while he's mid-tour in Tibet.
